Top Down Shooter Game

Contains:
- Player and enemy animations:
    - Player has 4 key frames for left, right, up, and down for the movement
    - Enemy has 4 key frames for their entire movement
- Player movement:
    - The player can move all four directions including diagonal
    - The player's animation changes based on which direction it's going
- Rotating weapon for player + weapon shooting projectiles:
    - The weapon rotates 360 degrees around the player and can shoot projectiles.
    - The projectiles disappear once it collides with an enemy or once it reaches the time limit that I set
- Win and lose screen:
    - If the player wins or loses, a screen will pop up that says "You Win" or "You Lose"
    - The player wins by completing the last wave, the player loses when it makes contact with an enemy
- Wave system that spawns more and more enemies after each wave:
    - The wave system spawns +3 enemies after each wave, can have 3 waves or 100 waves, the amount will add 3 after each wave.
- Terrain with collision:
    - The map was created on the tiled software, added collision to objects and the boundary so that the player and enemies don't phase through objects and the map
- Sound effects such as bullet collision, music, and shooting:
    - Background music for the game along with a sound that players after each bullet that shoots along with every bullet collision with an enemy.
- Camera movement with player:
    - The camera moves with the player, keeping the player in the center of the screen
- Enemy tracking player:
    - The enemies have a simple logic where they chase the player at a set speed, once contacted with the player, the player loses and the game is over.
